Workmanship Warranty

Last updated: July 15, 2026

We stand behind our work. Elite is in our name because it is the standard we hold ourselves to, and this page explains what that means once the truck pulls away.

What Is Covered

Gonzalez Elite Contractor warrants the workmanship on the projects we complete. If a defect in how we performed the work appears within the warranty period stated on your written estimate or invoice, we will come back and correct it at no charge. This covers our installation and construction work, such as forming, placing, finishing, and the site prep and base work underneath.

About Concrete and Cracking

Every concrete slab develops some hairline cracking as it cures and as the ground moves. That is the nature of the material, and Central Texas clay soil is hard on it. Cracks that are cosmetic and within normal industry tolerance are not defects. Structural cracking, separation, or settling caused by our forming, pouring, or base preparation is covered, and we will tell you honestly which one you are looking at.

What Is Not Covered

  • Normal hairline curing cracks and the natural aging of materials over time
  • Soil movement, drought, drainage changes, and tree roots beyond our control
  • Damage from storms, floods, fire, vehicles, or heavy equipment the slab was not designed for
  • Damage caused by alterations, misuse, or work performed by others after we finish
  • Issues arising from materials, plans, or engineering supplied by the customer
  • Materials covered instead by a manufacturer's warranty, which we will help you pursue
  • Pre-existing conditions we identified and documented before starting

Making a Warranty Claim

Call or text us at (512) 554-4055, or email edgargonzalez69@icloud.com. Describe the issue and send photos if you can. We will come take a look and make it right if it falls under this warranty.

Liability

Our responsibility for any project is limited to repairing or correcting our own workmanship as described above. We carry out every job with care, we work carefully around what stays, and we clean up the site when we are done.

Licensing

Texas does not issue a statewide license for general contracting, concrete, or demolition work. We pull permits where a job requires them and we work to local code. If your project needs a licensed trade we do not perform, we will tell you up front.

These warranty terms are a plain-language summary, not legal advice. The written estimate or agreement for your specific project controls if there is any difference between the two. Read the liability and warranty clauses closely, and have an attorney review them if you want certainty.
