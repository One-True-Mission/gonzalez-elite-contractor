Accessibility Statement

Last updated: July 15, 2026

Gonzalez Elite Contractor wants every person who visits this site to be able to use it, including people who browse with a screen reader, a keyboard, magnification, or reduced motion turned on.

Our Goal

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA. That is the standard we build and check against.

What We Have Done

  • Descriptive alt text on the photographs, so screen readers can describe the work
  • Semantic headings in logical order on every page
  • Text and background colors chosen for contrast, including the gold on dark
  • Full keyboard access to the navigation, the carousel controls, and the estimate form
  • Labeled form fields, so it is clear what each one is asking for
  • Support for reduced motion: if your device requests it, the scrolling strip and the auto-advancing gallery stop moving
  • A responsive layout that works on a phone, a tablet, or a desktop

Ongoing Effort

Accessibility is not a box we check once. As we add project photos and update the site, we keep working at it. Parts of the site may still fall short, and we would rather hear about it than not.

Hit a Barrier? Tell Us

If any part of this site kept you from finding what you needed, call or text (512) 554-4055, or email edgargonzalez69@icloud.com. Tell us what happened and we will fix it. We will also get you the information another way, right then, so a website problem never costs you your estimate.

You can always reach us by phone or text for anything on this site. Everything here is available that way too.
