Residential & Commercial

Concrete, Demo & Site Work

Six services, one crew, one number to call. Here is exactly what we do and how we do it.

What We Handle

The Whole Job, Not Half of It

Concrete work rarely stands alone. Something has to come out, the ground has to be right, and water has to go somewhere. We cover all of it so you are not coordinating three different crews.

Driveways, Patios & Slabs

New concrete flatwork poured on a base that is actually prepped for it. Driveways, patios, sidewalks, and slabs, formed square, poured at the right thickness, and finished the way you want it.

  • Driveways and approaches
  • Patios and walkways
  • Sidewalks and steps
  • Slabs for additions, shops, and sheds

Concrete Removal & Replacement

Concrete cracks for a reason, and pouring over the problem just buys you a year. We break out the old, haul it off, correct the base underneath, and replace it so the new slab has a fighting chance.

  • Cracked, sunken, and spalling concrete
  • Full break out and haul off
  • Base repair before the new pour
  • Tear out only, if that is all you need

Residential & Commercial Demolition

Interior tear-outs, slabs, structures, and full commercial demo. The skill in demolition is knowing what stays. We work carefully around it, then haul everything off and leave the site clean.

  • Interior demo and gut-outs
  • Slab, driveway, and wall removal
  • Structure and outbuilding teardown
  • Commercial demolition

Site Prep, Grading & Drainage

The part nobody sees and everything depends on. We clear, grade, and prep the ground so it is build-ready, and we route water away from your foundation instead of letting it pond against it.

  • Site preparation and cleanup
  • Grading and leveling
  • Drainage and water routing
  • Lot clearing and haul off

House Leveling & Pier and Beam

Sloping floors, doors that stick, cracks that keep coming back. Those usually start under the house. We level the structure and repair pier and beam foundations to bring the house back true.

  • House and structure leveling
  • Pier and beam repair
  • Beam and joist replacement
  • Free assessment before any work

Retaining Walls & Stucco

Walls that hold back a slope and still look good from the yard, plus stucco finishes on walls and foundations. We do custom stone work too, from tree rings to accent walls.

  • Block and concrete retaining walls
  • Stucco on walls and foundations
  • Custom stone work
  • Decorative and structural both
