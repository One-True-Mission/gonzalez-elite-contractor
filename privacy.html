Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 15, 2026

Gonzalez Elite Contractor ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect through this website, why we collect it, and what you can do about it. In plain language: we collect what we need to give you an estimate, and nothing else.

What We Collect

When you submit the estimate form on this site, we collect the information you type into it:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address, if you provide one
  • Your city or service area
  • The service you selected and the project details you describe

We do not run analytics or advertising trackers on this site, and we do not set tracking cookies. Our web host records standard server logs, such as IP addresses and page requests, as part of delivering the site.

How We Use It

We use your information to respond to your request, schedule and prepare your free estimate, and communicate with you about your project. That is the whole list.

What We Do Not Do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not rent, trade, or share it with advertisers or data brokers, and we do not add you to marketing lists you did not ask to join.

Who Else Touches Your Information

Two service providers handle your information as part of running this site:

  • Formspree processes the estimate form and delivers it to our email inbox.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and keeps standard server logs.

Each has its own privacy policy governing how it handles data. We share your information with them only to the extent needed to operate the site and reach you back.

How Long We Keep It

We keep estimate requests as long as we need them to serve you and maintain our business records. You can ask us to delete yours at any time.

Your Texas Privacy Rights

Under the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act (TDPSA), Texas residents have the right to:

  • Confirm whether we are processing your personal data and access it
  • Correct inaccuracies in your personal data
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Obtain a copy of the personal data you provided to us
  • Opt out of the sale of personal data, of targeted advertising, and of profiling (note that we do none of these)

To exercise any of these rights, call or text us at (512) 554-4055 or email edgargonzalez69@icloud.com. We will respond within the timeframe the law requires. If we deny your request, you may appeal by contacting us at the same number or address.

Children

This site is meant for adults arranging contracting work. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact Us

Questions about your privacy or this policy? Call or text (512) 554-4055, or email edgargonzalez69@icloud.com.

This policy is a plain-language summary provided for transparency. It is not legal advice. If you need certainty about how a specific privacy law applies to you, consult an attorney.
