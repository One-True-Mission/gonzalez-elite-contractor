Concrete Work Done The Elite Way

Most concrete jobs are really two jobs. Something has to come out before anything goes in, and the ground underneath has to be right or the new slab cracks. Gonzalez Elite does both. We demo the old, grade and prep the base, then pour and finish it clean.

That means one crew, one number to call, and no finger-pointing between a demo outfit and a concrete outfit. Residential or commercial, we show up, do the work, and clean up after ourselves.
