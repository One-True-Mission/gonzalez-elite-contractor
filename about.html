Who We Are

A Round Rock Crew That Does Both Halves

Gonzalez Elite Contractor is a concrete and demolition company based in Round Rock, working across greater Austin. We got into this trade from the demolition side, and that shaped how we do concrete. We have seen what is under a lot of failed slabs, and it is almost never the concrete's fault. It is the prep.

So we built the company to handle the whole job. Demo, haul off, grading, drainage, and the pour, with one crew accountable from the first swing to the final finish. No handoffs, no one blaming the other guy when something settles.

The work runs from custom stone tree rings to commercial demolition. Different scale, same standard. Every job gets a free estimate and a straight answer about what it will actually take.

What Elite Means Here

Not a slogan. These are the three things we will not cut corners on.

Prep Before Pour

Anyone can pour concrete. The job is what happens before that: breaking out the old, grading the base, and making sure water has somewhere to go. Skip it and the slab tells on you in two years.

A Straight Number

Free estimates, in writing, after we have actually seen the site. If the job is bigger than you hoped, we tell you before you commit, not halfway through with your driveway already broken out.

We Clean Up

Demolition makes a mess. That mess is our responsibility, not yours. We haul it off and leave the site clean, because the finished result includes what the place looks like when we drive away.
